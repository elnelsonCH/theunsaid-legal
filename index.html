The Unsaid

The Unsaid is a YouTube channel of video essays about the psychology of money. This page hosts the privacy policy for the internal tool the channel uses to manage its own YouTube account.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

This application is used only by the owner of The Unsaid to upload videos, captions and thumbnails, manage comments and read analytics for the channel's own YouTube account, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from any other user. YouTube data accessed by the tool is used only to manage the owner's channel and is not shared with third parties.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
